How do I install SABR on Android?
  1. Tap Download for Android above to download the APK on your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.
  3. Install SABR, then turn off that installation permission if you no longer need it. Only install the file from a source you trust.
What about iPhone and iPad?

iOS availability will be announced on this page. The Android APK cannot be installed on an iPhone or iPad.

Privacy and AI

This download page requires no account and contains no analytics or advertising scripts. SABR's core progress and plans are stored on your device. If you choose to use Gemini-powered features, relevant information and messages are sent to Google for processing, even when local chat saving is off. The AI is optional and is not a scholar or a replacement for professional support. Keep any exported backups private.

Will my progress be kept when I update?

Before installing an update, export a backup in SABR and store it securely. Install a compatible update over the existing app rather than uninstalling it. If Android refuses the update, keep your current installation and backup until you can safely migrate.
